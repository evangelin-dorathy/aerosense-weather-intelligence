Predictive Weather Intelligence

AeroSense utilizes machine learning models trained on over a decade of weather history observations from Szeged, Hungary. Explore historical patterns and forecast next-day conditions dynamically.

Predictive Architecture

  • Rainfall Classifier Predicts if it will rain using temperature, apparent temperature, humidity, pressure, and wind speed features.
  • Next-Day Temp Forecaster Uses daily aggregated time-series lag features (T-1, T-2, T-3) to regress next-day average temperature.
  • Weather Classifier Multi-class classification categorizing weather into 4 major conditions: Clear, Cloudy, Rainy, or Foggy.

Szegedi Weather Dataset

Szeged is one of the sunniest cities in Hungary, often called the "City of Sunshine". The historical dataset spanning 2006-2016 contains hourly records of:
